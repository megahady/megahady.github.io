Academic Profile

Electrical engineer and robotics researcher with a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and interdisciplinary experience in intelligent control, machine learning, mechatronics, robotics, wearable systems, and biomedical engineering. Research focuses on learning-based control, physics-informed modeling and simulation, and AI-based signal decoding for complex physical systems.

Research Areas

Learning-Based Control

Machine learning, reinforcement learning, neural networks, and adaptive control for intelligent and personalized physical systems.

Physics-Informed Control & Simulation

Integration of physical models, system dynamics, and data-driven methods for control and high-fidelity simulation.

AI-Based Signal Decoding

AI methods for decoding EMG, ECG, EEG, and other biosignals for human-machine interaction and intelligent control.

Technical Expertise

Programming & Computational Tools: Experienced with Python, MATLAB, C++, and C# for scientific computing, modeling, simulation, data analysis, control development, and robotic-system implementation.
Modeling & Simulation: Modeling and simulation of electrochemical and electromechanical systems, including development, analysis, and validation of dynamic system models.
Experimental Design & Validation: Experimental design, data acquisition, quantitative analysis, performance evaluation, and validation of computational models and experimental results.
Hardware–Software Co-Design: Hardware–software co-design and system integration for reliable, real-time mechatronic and robotic systems, with emphasis on system integrity and interaction among hardware, software, sensing, and control.
Biomedical Signal Processing: Analysis and decoding of biomedical biosignals, including EMG, ECG, EEG, and other physiological signals, for system identification, monitoring, control, and human-machine interaction.
