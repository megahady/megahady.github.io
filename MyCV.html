Academic Profile

Electrical engineer and biomedical AI researcher with a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and interdisciplinary experience in machine learning, deep learning, biomedical signal analysis, image-based recognition, intelligent systems, mechatronics, robotics, and translational medical technologies. Research experience includes AI-based analysis of physiological data, deep-learning methods for biomedical applications, computational modeling, and development of algorithms for real-world medical and assistive systems. Current research includes AI-based image analysis for assessing blood-cell degradation and estimating blood-product freshness, with emphasis on computational optimization and translation toward medical-device deployment.
