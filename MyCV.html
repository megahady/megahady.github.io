Academic Profile

Electrical engineer and robotics researcher with a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and interdisciplinary research experience spanning intelligent control, machine learning, mechatronics, robotics, wearable systems, prosthetics, exoskeletons, and biomedical engineering. My research focuses on developing intelligent methods for controlling and understanding complex physical systems by integrating learning-based approaches, physics-informed models, simulation, and AI-based signal decoding. I have conducted interdisciplinary research with the National Institutes of Health, Cleveland Clinic, U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, Ford Motor Company, and Siemens. My long-term research goal is to develop adaptive and intelligent physical systems that can learn from data while maintaining the interpretability, reliability, and physical consistency required for real-world deployment.

Research Areas

Learning-Based Control

Development of intelligent control strategies that combine machine learning, reinforcement learning, neural networks, and adaptive control to enable personalized and autonomous control of complex physical systems.

Physics-Informed Control & Simulation

Integration of physical knowledge, mathematical models, system dynamics, and data-driven methods to develop physics-informed controllers and high-fidelity simulation environments for complex mechatronic and robotic systems.

AI-Based Signal Decoding

Development of artificial intelligence and machine-learning methods for decoding physiological and sensor signals, including EMG, ECG, EEG, and other biosignals, for human-machine interaction, system identification, and intelligent control.
