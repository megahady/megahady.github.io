Academic Profile

Electrical engineer and robotics researcher with a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and interdisciplinary research experience spanning mechatronics, adaptive control, machine learning, wearable robotics, prosthetics, exoskeletons, and biomedical engineering. Established research direction in adaptive intelligence for physical robotic systems, with experience developing personalized control algorithms, sensor-driven systems, and real-time robotic technologies. Research experience includes collaborations with the National Institutes of Health, Cleveland Clinic, U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, Ford Motor Company, and Siemens. Prepared to contribute to undergraduate and graduate education in mechatronics, robotics, control systems, automation, and intelligent systems while developing an externally visible interdisciplinary research program.
